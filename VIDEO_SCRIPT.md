## 📜 Scene-by-Scene Director's Script

### Scene 1: Hook & The Editorial Vision (0:00 – 0:25)
* **URL**: `http://127.0.0.1:5001/`
* **Visual Actions**:
  1. Start on the homepage top hero.
  2. Slowly scroll down to reveal the headline: *"Engineering careers built on transparent intelligence, not recruiter noise"*.
  3. Click one of the theme palette pills in the top bar (e.g., switch between Cream and Midnight or Slate) to demonstrate dynamic styling.
  4. Bring mouse smoothly down toward the live feed counter.

* **Voiceover Script (Speak naturally)**:
  > *"Most job portals today are flooded with automated spam, outdated postings, and zero salary transparency. This is WorkLane — a reimagined career platform engineered for software engineers and high-growth tech companies. Built with a clean, editorial broadsheet aesthetic, WorkLane eliminates the noise so engineers can focus strictly on what matters: verified opportunities and real transparency."*

### Scene 2: Intelligent Discovery & Real-Time Filtering (0:25 – 0:50)

  2. Click the **"Filters" toggle button** to smoothly reveal the collapsible filter drawer.

## 💡 Quick Tips for a 10/10 Recording
1. **Mouse Movement**: Move your mouse deliberately and smoothly. Avoid frantic circles or rapid clicks.
2. **Browser Window**: Keep browser bookmarks hidden (`Cmd + Shift + B` on Mac) for a clean, full-screen look.
3. **Pacing**: Take a 1-second breath between scenes. You have plenty of time (24 seconds per scene) to speak clearly without rushing.
